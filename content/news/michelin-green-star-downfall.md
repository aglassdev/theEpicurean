Earlier this year, Michelin announced that they would be ending the Green Star award after a six-year stint as one of the most coveted awards for sustainable gastronomy. Over the past half decade, we have seen some restaurants build their entire identity around the green leaf, while others—usually 3-star establishments—have tacked it on as an extra accolade, an extra star. Almost 600 restaurants were given the award worldwide, which on the surface looks like a testament to Michelin's commitment to supporting sustainability, the reality is quite a bit bleaker, and if I may say, fundamentally flawed.

Now, don't get me wrong, I am all for sustainability. The restaurant industry is by nature wasteful, and while zero waste is a fallacy, it is important to recognize those establishments that make an effort to cut down on their byproduct. But take a second and just think about the irony of a green star: A three star restaurant is defined as one "worth a special journey," meaning wealthy individuals should travel a long ways just to eat at a restaurant. Now you cannot tell me that the waste caused by a plane and car ride is offset by a composted quail bone or a leek tip turned into stock.

But even if we were to forgo the fundamental irony of it, there are also flaws in the way they award it. Michelin inspectors have always and will always be anonymous, and while that grants the important impartiality from restaurants, it also doesn't give them access to look at the restaurant beyond its dining experience and any media—which may or may not be biased. As a result, these restaurants can't be audited, thus they resort to an online survey, filled out at the restauranteur's discretion, with no checks or balances.

The Guide is replacing the Green Star with a new content platform—thought up by suits who don't understand social media, with a splash of AI—called "Mindful Voices." I have to hand it to the tire boys for this one, this might be the stupidest, most cliche name for a social media site I've ever heard. Mindful Voices goal is apparenlty to highlight those individuals who are committed to sustainability. Revolutionary. Besides the fact nobody will use this website, a paragraph long shoutout from a random food blogger is not really as alluring as a coveted award from the most prestigious company in the culinary scene.

At the time of writing this, Mindful Voices has been out for a little over two months. So far, has been a page of uninspiring garbage, with half the stories not even related to sustainability. Don't worry, the Green Star won't completely disappear. There will always be a record of every awarded restaurant here for your perusal in The Epicurean.
